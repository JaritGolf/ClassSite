# Draft — Questions for the Educational Technology Department

> ## ⚠️ DRAFT. NOT SENT. NOT FILED.
> Nothing in this file has been transmitted to the district. Sending it is a deliberate,
> separate decision by the owner. Drafted 2026-08-07.

**Why send it at all.** SDPBC Board Policy 3.29 requires Technology Clearinghouse approval before
an employee uses a non-District-approved product, and Policy 5.50 §12(k) separately contemplates
services that students are "referred to … but are not required to use." Nothing published
reconciles the two, and the second one places its duty on the School Board rather than on a
teacher. That is a genuine gap on the public record, not a loophole — and the district's own
reading of it is worth more than any interpretation reached independently.

**Why ask before submitting.** Questions 2 and 3 determine who files the PBSD 2199 and when. It
is cheaper to learn that a principal must submit it than to file it wrong.

**Contact of record:** Rebecca Smykla, Director of Educational Technology, (561) 969-5878.
Alternative: IT Service Desk, itservicedesk@palmbeachschools.org.

## Notes for the owner before sending

- **Question 4 is the one that matters most for the next few weeks.** It is the difference
  between having a usable classroom option this term and having none.
- **Question 7 has a real edge.** All 371 active board policies were enumerated and there is no
  outside-employment, IP, or work-for-hire policy; Policy 8.121 covers only *using others'*
  copyrighted work. Ownership would fall to the employment contract, the collective bargaining
  agreement, and general work-for-hire law. The site is already live at a personally-owned
  domain, so this is worth settling regardless of the Clearinghouse outcome.
- **Do not raise a filter allowlist exception.** Policy 3.29 separately forbids circumventing
  content filters, and asking would signal use ahead of approval. Reachability is not permission.
- **Keep monetization out of the conversation entirely.** Policy 3.29 bars using district
  technology for financial gain, and § 112.313(3), F.S. bars a public employee acting privately
  from selling goods or services to their own political subdivision. An unmonetized tool that
  benefits the district appears to fit 3.29's own carve-out; introducing money changes the
  analysis and would need a current Commission on Ethics opinion.
- **Close `DEMO_OPEN_LOGIN` before giving anyone the URL.** Any visitor can currently sign in as
  an administrator.
